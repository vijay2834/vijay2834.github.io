01 · About

About me

I'm a Master's student in Data Science at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My applied research is on building AI applications for fintech, specifically intelligent multi-agent systems that automate credit fraud risk reporting.

Before Madison I spent three years as a Senior Analyst in Data Science at American Express. I built ML models on fraud transaction data, segmented customer friction across payment channels, and delivered dashboards that changed servicing strategy across 400K+ monthly transactions.

My background is in NLP. At the University of East Anglia I researched gender bias in transformer models, which led to a review paper in Elsevier's Natural Language Processing Journal.

I like work where rigorous modeling meets production systems, from GNNs and LLM fine-tuning to Spark pipelines and Kubernetes.
